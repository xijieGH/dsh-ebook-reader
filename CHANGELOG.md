# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-27

Initial release.

- Left-sidebar **E-books** panel with a local bookshelf.
- EPUB 2/3 import from the file picker or drag and drop, with cover extraction, duplicate detection, search, sorting, title/author editing and removal.
- Reader with nested table of contents, chapter navigation, font size, light/sepia/dark/auto reading themes and saved reading positions.
- Last-opened book and reading position survive panel switches and DSH restarts.
- Books, progress and settings persist under `$DSH_HOME/storages/ebook-reader/`.
- English and Chinese interface.
